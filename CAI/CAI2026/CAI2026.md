# CAI 2026: CWT AI Forum

CAI (crush on AI) is the student AI forum organized by the student club CWT. This forum marks the final milestone of our year-long journey transitioning from learning AI fundamentals to conducting independent, academic-style research intersecting biological sciences and artificial intelligence.

---

## 📌 About CWT
**CWT** is a student AI club dedicated to learning, research, and collaboration. Our mission is to empower members to:
- Learn core AI fundamentals and structured workflows.
- Explore diverse advanced machine learning fields.
- Develop distinct scientific research interests.
- Conduct independent and team-based computational projects.
- Share research outcomes with the broader scientific community.

---

## 🗓️ The Research Journey

### Semester 1: Building Foundations
During the first semester, members focused on establishing robust theoretical and practical machine learning capabilities through a structured **Group Study System** guided by mentors.
- **Dedicated Study Groups**: Tabular Data Machine Learning, Computer Vision (CV), Time Series Analysis, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Reinforcement Learning (RL).
- **Core Theory Sessions**:
  - ML Basics, Data Types, and ML Workflows
  - Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)
  - Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)
- **Self-Directed Learning**: Strengthening technical depth via distributed online resources, paper reading, and independent coding assignments.

### Semester 2: Preparing for Research
The second semester shifted focus from guided learning to active scientific inquiry and project execution:
- Critically reading, breaking down, and reproducing methods from peer-reviewed AI & bioinformatics research papers.
- Exploring personal research interests and identifying domain gaps.
- Forming collaborative research teams based on shared domain visions.
- Designing, implementing, validating, and benchmarking independent self-projects.

---

## 🔬 What is CAI?
**CAI (CWT AI Forum)** is our signature student research forum bridging biological sciences and AI technologies. It provides a formal platform to:
- Present independent research project outcomes.
- Share novel methodological ideas, datasets, and pipelines.
- Receive rigorous constructive feedback from peers and mentors.
- Experience authentic, professional academic-style research communication.

### Research Structure
- **Members**: Formulated unique individual or team research topics, conducted extensive computational experiments, and finalized analytical pipelines under mentor guidance.
- **Mentors**: Provided technical and domain advisement on member projects, drove their own advanced research pipelines, and participated in collaborative advising sessions.
